Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue helps animals in need find safe and loving homes. We work with our community to rescue, care for, and support animals and families.

How You Can Help

There are many ways to support animals in our community. You can adopt a pet, become a foster family, volunteer your time, or help by making a donation.

Our Impact

Hundreds of animals have been helped through rescue, foster care, and adoption.

Our volunteers give their time to help animals receive the care and attention they need.

Our community partners help us connect animals with safe and loving homes.
